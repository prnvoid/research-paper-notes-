# Research Paper Notes

A curated collection of research notes, paper analyses, and technical explorations in recommender systems, music recommendation, personalization, and information discovery.

The repository documents key concepts, methodologies, findings, and limitations from academic research, with an emphasis on understanding recommendation approaches and their applications to music discovery.

## Research Areas

- **Recommender Systems:** Collaborative filtering, sequential recommendation, and personalized ranking.
- **Music Recommendation:** Music discovery, user preferences, and recommendation strategies.
- **Serendipity & Discovery:** Exploring how recommendation systems can facilitate relevant and unexpected discoveries.
- **Representation Learning:** Embeddings, sequential models, and user-item interactions.
- **Personalization:** User modeling, preference representation, and recommendation quality.
